Yatharth SharmaAI Video Editor & AI UGC Ads Specialist · Published 29 Oct 2026

The short answer

There are three ways to get AI UGC ads, and the price depends on how much of the work you do yourself: AI tools (cheapest, but you do the creative work), a freelance AI video editor (paid by the hour or project, delivers ready-to-run ads) or an agency (monthly retainer).

Why the cheapest option isn't always the cheapest

AI tools make the video itself cheap. What they don't do is decide what the ad should say. A $5 AI video with a weak hook still wastes your ad spend. Most of the value is in:

  • The hook: the first 2 seconds decide whether anyone keeps watching.
  • Realism: natural hands, faces, lip-sync and product shots, so the ad doesn't feel "AI".
  • Variations: several hooks and angles so you can find a winner.
  • Ad-ready editing: captions, pacing and a clear call to action for Meta and TikTok.

How to get the most out of your budget

  1. Start with a small batch of ads and several hook variations, not one perfect video.
  2. Test, keep the winners, then make more ads in the same style.
  3. Send a clear brief: product, offer, audience and reference ads you like.
