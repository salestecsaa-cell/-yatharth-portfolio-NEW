FAQ

What is a performance video ad?

A performance video ad is a direct-response ad made to drive a measurable action, such as a purchase, sign-up or install. It is judged on results like hook rate, click-through rate and cost per acquisition, so it is built to be tested and iterated rather than made once.

Can you edit footage I already have?

Yes. I can cut ads from your product footage, creator UGC, screen recordings or existing ads, and add AI-generated scenes, voiceover or B-roll where the footage runs short.

How do hook variations work?

I keep the body of a winning ad and swap the first seconds: a different opening line, visual, caption or pattern interrupt. You launch the variations side by side and keep the ones with the best hook rate and cost per result.

Which formats do you deliver?

Vertical 9:16 for Instagram and Facebook Reels, TikTok and YouTube Shorts, and 16:9 for YouTube and landing pages. Other aspect ratios are available on request.
