What is an AI UGC ad?

An AI UGC ad is a user-generated-content style video ad made with AI: a realistic AI creator shows, uses or talks about your product in the casual, phone-shot style that performs well on Meta and TikTok, without hiring a real creator or organizing a shoot.

Which platforms and formats do you deliver?
